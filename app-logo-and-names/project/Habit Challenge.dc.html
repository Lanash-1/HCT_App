Android · Material 3

One does the work.
One does the watching.

The asymmetry is the product. A challenger commits to a short stack of daily habits; a witness — who is not competing — sees every check-in and every miss, and is the only person who can nudge. Accountability without a leaderboard.

Assumptions
A challenge holds several habits — capped at five, so the day stays winnable.
The day counts as complete only when every habit is checked. Partial days are visible, not hidden.
Streaks belong to each habit; perfect days belong to the challenge.
Role is a mode, not an account. The same person can run a challenge and witness two others.
A miss is visible to exactly one person — the witness — not a feed.
Screens
1 · Pairing & role — onboarding
2 · Create a challenge — multiple habits
3 · Today — the daily stack
4 · Weekly recap, shareable
5 · Profile — switch challenger ⇄ witness
6 · Watching — witness mode home
7 · Witness detail — right-hand phone, live
Both phones share one state. Check habits off on the left and the right updates; cheer or nudge on the right and the left responds.
Next steps
Dark theme is in — Profile → Appearance flips both phones, and the tweak panel sets the default. Photo-proof capture, per-habit reminder times, and the streak-broken recovery moment are the remaining follow-ons.
